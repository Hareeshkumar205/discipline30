Weekly plan

💚
Beginner recovery rule

Strength train three days per week at first. Keep weights manageable, learn form, and use the other days for easy walking or recovery. Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms.

30-day roadmap

Days 1–7Build the cue and learn the routine.
Days 8–14Repeat; don't rush to increase weights.
Days 15–21Practise returning quickly after a missed day.
Days 22–30Review what you can sustain long-term.
